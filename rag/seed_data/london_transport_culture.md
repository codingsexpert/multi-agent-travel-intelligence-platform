# London Transportation, Historic Sites & Commuting Customs

London combines historical pageantry with a sprawling public transit system operated by Transport for London (TfL).

## Tube Etiquette & Escalator Rules
When riding London Underground escalators, the rule is immutable: stand on the right, walk on the left. Blocking the left side will immediately draw quiet disapproval from hurried commuters. Always let passengers off the carriage before boarding trains.

## Contactless & Oyster Transit Payment
Physical paper transit tickets are obsolete for most visitors. Tap in and tap out at station fare gates using a contactless credit/debit card, Apple Pay, or a rechargeable Oyster card. Daily fare capping automatically limits your total daily transit spend across Zones 1 and 2.

## Free National Museum Admissions
Permanent collections at premier London museums—including the British Museum, National Gallery, Tate Modern, Science Museum, and Natural History Museum—are legally free to all visitors. Advance online entry booking is recommended to skip exterior queues.
