# Rome Cultural Etiquette, Vatican Guidance & Heritage Rules

The Eternal City pairs millennia of open-air architectural wonders with distinct regional dining and sacred monument customs.

## Vatican & Church Dress Code
Strict dress standards are enforced at St. Peter's Basilica, the Vatican Museums, and churches throughout Rome. Both men and women must cover their shoulders and knees. Sleeveless tops, miniskirts, and hats inside sanctuaries are prohibited; guards will deny entry.

## Coffee Customs & Breakfast Etiquette
In Italian coffee culture, milk-based coffees like cappuccino, caffe latte, and macchiato are enjoyed exclusively in the morning before 11:00 AM. Ordering a cappuccino after a pasta meal is considered unusual by Italian baristas. Drink espresso quickly at the counter (al banco) for the classic Roman cadence and cheaper price.

## Historic Fountain Protection
Rome's historic fountains (including the Trevi Fountain and Piazza Navona fountains) are protected cultural treasures. Sitting on the rim of the Trevi fountain, eating or drinking on fountain steps, or touching the water will result in immediate heavy municipal fines.
