# Tokyo Landmark Experiences & Heritage Sites

Tokyo's distinct neighborhoods each possess their own architectural personality and historical gravity, ranging from serene Edo-period sanctuaries to cutting-edge digital exhibitions.

## Senso-ji Temple & Asakusa Historic Quarter
Founded in 645 AD, Senso-ji is Tokyo's oldest and most revered Buddhist temple, dedicated to the Bodhisattva Kannon. Visitors enter through the iconic Kaminarimon (Thunder Gate) and traverse Nakamise-dori, a shopping lane offering traditional senbei rice crackers and folding fans. Best visited in the early morning before crowds gather.

## Meiji Jingu Shrine & Harajuku Forest
Constructed in 1920 to honor Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken, this serene Shinto shrine is enveloped by an evergreen forest of over 100,000 trees donated from across Japan. Walk along the wide gravel pathways, observe the massive wooden Torii gate, and take part in the temizuya purification water ritual before approaching the main prayer hall.

## Tsukiji Outer Market & Toyosu Wholesale
While the inner wholesale fish market moved to modern Toyosu, the historic Tsukiji Outer Market remains a vibrant hub for culinary exploration. Over 400 compact stalls serve fresh nigiri, tamagoyaki omelettes, grilled wagyu skewers, and fresh matcha soft serve from 6:00 AM until early afternoon.

## teamLab Planets Digital Art Museum
Located in Toyosu, teamLab Planets is a world-renowned interactive digital art experience where visitors walk barefoot through knee-high water, immerse themselves in crystalline light installations, and enter floating orchid gardens. Advance timed-entry ticket reservations are strictly required.
