# New York City Travel Tips, Subways & Etiquette

New York City moves at an energetic pace. Following local etiquette helps travelers navigate Manhattan and the outer boroughs seamlessly.

## Sidewalk Etiquette
Never stop abruptly in the center of busy sidewalks to look at your smartphone or map; step to the side near building facades. Keep to the right side of stairs and walkways to maintain the flow of foot traffic.

## Tipping Standards
In American dining culture, tipping is standard practice and forms the primary compensation for service personnel. Expect to tip 18% to 22% of the pre-tax bill in sit-down restaurants, $1 to $2 per drink at cocktail bars, and 15% to 20% in yellow cabs.

## OMNY Subway Tap-and-Go
The MTA subway system utilizes OMNY contactless readers at every turnstile. Simply tap your credit card or smartphone to enter. The system features a 7-day fare cap: after 12 paid rides within a rolling 7-day window, all subsequent rides on the same card are completely free.
