# Paris Cultural Etiquette & Destination Guidance

Paris is structured into 20 spiral arrondissements along the River Seine. Adhering to French social courtesies transforms your journey from an outsider visit into an authentic cultural exchange.

## The 'Bonjour' Rule
Never approach a shopkeeper, waiter, museum attendant, or hotel desk without initiating the interaction with a polite "Bonjour, Madame" or "Bonjour, Monsieur". In French social etiquette, speaking directly without this greeting is considered abrupt and discourteous. In the evening, transition naturally to "Bonsoir".

## Dining Rituals & Asking for the Check
Dining in Parisian bistros is a leisurely experience where table turnaround is never rushed. Waiters will not bring the bill until explicitly requested to avoid making guests feel unwelcome. Simply catch your server's eye and ask: "L'addition, s'il vous plaît." Tipping is not required as a 15% service charge (service compris) is legally included, though rounding up the change or leaving 1 to 2 euros for attentive service is customary.

## Museum Reservations & Art Heritage
World-class institutions including the Musée du Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, and Sainte-Chapelle strictly mandate advance online reservations with timed entry slots. Book slots 2 to 4 weeks prior to travel, especially during peak spring and autumn seasons.
