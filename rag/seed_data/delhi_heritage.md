# Delhi Heritage Sites, Cultural Manners & Practical Logistics

Delhi encompasses centuries of Mughal and colonial history across its diverse historic settlements.

## Sacred Monuments & Footwear Etiquette
When entering Hindu temples, Sikh gurdwaras, and mosques (such as Jama Masjid, Bangla Sahib, and Akshardham), visitors must remove their footwear. Footwear holding counters are available at entrances. Shoulders and knees must be modestly covered, and head coverings (scarves or bandanas) are required at Sikh gurdwaras.

## Delhi Metro Rail Network
The Delhi Metro provides an ultra-clean, air-conditioned, and punctual transit corridor connecting New Delhi, Old Delhi, and the NCR. Front cars on every train are reserved exclusively for women passengers. Purchase rechargeable smart cards or tourist day passes to bypass token lines.

## Street Food & Water Hygiene
While Old Delhi (Chandni Chowk) boasts historic culinary legends, travelers should drink exclusively bottled or purified water with factory seals intact. Opt for freshly cooked, piping-hot dishes at bustling stalls with high local turnover.
